## 中文版

### VEX 机器人

**AI视觉与智能控制 | 项目制学习 | 动手创造**

学生将在真实项目中亲手搭建、编程、测试并不断优化 VEX V5 机器人。课程将机器人机械设计、编程、传感器、自动控制与 AI 视觉结合，让学生理解机器人如何**感知环境、做出决策并执行行动**。

### 学生将学习

- 搭建与维护 VEX V5 机器人
- VEXcode / Python 编程
- 电机、传感器与自动控制
- 工程设计、测试与系统调试
- 机器人挑战与团队协作
- OpenCV / AI视觉，让机器人“看见并行动”

### AI 深度融入课程

学生通过真实机器人理解 AI 系统的核心链路：

**感知 → 决策 → 行动 → 测试 → 改进**

学生将学习：

- 摄像头和传感器如何把现实世界转化为数据
- 程序如何根据数据做出决策
- OpenCV 如何识别颜色、轮廓和目标位置
- 为什么视觉系统会受到光线、遮挡和环境影响
- 如何测试并提高智能系统的可靠性
- 如何负责任地使用 AI 编程工具辅助设计和调试

### 能力培养

- 工程思维
- 编程基础
- 问题解决
- 创造力与协作
- AI 素养

### 为什么学习这门课

- **真正的动手工程：** 学生不是只看机器人，而是亲手搭建、编程、测试和改进。
- **机器人 + AI：** 将机械、传感器、控制程序与 AI 视觉结合，创造更智能的机器人。
- **为未来 STEM 学习打基础：** 为机器人竞赛、工程、计算机科学和 AI 学习建立扎实基础。

## English Version

### VEX Robotics

**AI Vision & Intelligent Control | Project-Based Learning | Hands-On Innovation**

Students build, program, test, and improve real VEX V5 robots through hands-on projects. The course combines mechanical design, programming, sensors, autonomous control, and AI vision so students understand how robots **sense, decide, and act**.

### Students will learn

- Build and maintain VEX V5 robots
- Program with VEXcode / Python
- Motors, sensors, and autonomous control
- Engineering design, testing, and systematic debugging
- Robot challenges and teamwork
- OpenCV / AI vision so robots can see and act

### AI Is Embedded Throughout the Course

Students learn the core intelligent-system cycle through real robots:

**Perceive → Decide → Act → Test → Improve**

Students learn:

- how cameras and sensors turn the physical world into data;
- how programs make decisions from that data;
- how OpenCV can detect colors, contours, and target position;
- why vision systems can fail under changing lighting, occlusion, and environments;
- how engineers test and improve system reliability;
- how to use AI coding tools responsibly for design and debugging.

### Skills Developed

- Engineering thinking
- Coding fundamentals
- Problem solving
- Creativity and teamwork
- AI literacy

### Why This Course Matters

- **Real hands-on engineering:** Students build, program, test, debug, and improve physical systems.
- **Robotics + AI:** Students combine mechanics, sensing, control, and AI vision to create smarter robots.
- **Future STEM preparation:** The course builds foundations for robotics competitions, engineering, computer science, and AI learning.
