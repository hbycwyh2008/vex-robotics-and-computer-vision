1. Ask students to predict.
2. Show one controlled case.
3. Ask students to describe the evidence.
4. Explain the underlying concept.
5. Change one variable and compare.

## Test / Debug / Redesign / Retest

Students must use evidence rather than guessing.

Record:

- what was tested;
- what happened;
- what failed or varied;
- the team's hypothesis;
- the change made;
- the retest result.

For reliability, accuracy, or improvement claims, require repeated trials.

## Engineering Notebook Evidence

Every major work session should leave team evidence.

Minimum required before leaving class:

- [ ] today's goal;
- [ ] one design/build/code change;
- [ ] one test or observation;
- [ ] one result, measurement, or failure;
- [ ] one next step.

For challenge/project lessons also require:

- [ ] debugging evidence;
- [ ] justified design decision;
- [ ] retest or comparison data.

## Explain / Share / Transfer

Students explain the result, defend a decision, compare approaches, or transfer the same concept to a new engineering constraint.

Prompt examples:

- Why did the system behave this way?
- Which evidence changed your decision?
- What would you change if the constraint changed?
- Could you rebuild this pattern without the full instructions?
