## 85–90 min — Reset and Exit Ticket

### Physical Reset

- robot powered down if appropriate;
- battery handled according to classroom procedure;
- parts/tools returned;
- cables protected;
- floor/test area cleared;
- notebook/evidence saved.

### Exit Ticket

One technical question that reveals whether students understand the lesson rather than merely completed the build/code.

Possible question types:

- explain why a failure occurred;
- identify the next valid test;
- predict behavior after one variable changes;
- describe the core pattern;
- state current Mastery Level and supporting evidence.

---

## Differentiation

### Support

- reduce the number of variables;
- provide a labeled diagram or partially completed data table;
- isolate one subsystem;
- pair physical observation with pseudocode before code;
- provide a short rebuild checklist;
- reduce the number of simultaneous hardware/code changes.

### Extension

- require additional trials;
- optimize a second metric;
- quantify error;
- compare two implementations;
- remove the checklist and require independent rebuild;
- transfer the pattern to a new challenge;
- use the competition extension track.

## Teacher Reflection After Class

- What consumed more time than planned?
- Which misconception appeared repeatedly?
- Which hardware failure needs preventive maintenance?
- Which teams need reteaching?
- Which students showed stronger/weaker individual mastery than the team result suggested?
- Did notebook evidence actually drive decisions, or was it written after the fact?
- Should the next meeting remain on this anchor lesson or advance?
