# Differentiation and Team Roles

Different experience changes scaffolding, not whether a student builds, codes, tests and explains.

## Rotating roles
Build lead; Programming lead; Test lead; Evidence lead. For teams of two, combine and rotate. Do not permit a permanent programmer/permanent parts assistant split.

## Support
Subsystem diagrams; pseudocode before syntax; small labeled part sets; fewer experimental variables; partial data tables; verbal prediction before coding; known-good baseline comparison; one-subsystem assessment.

## Advanced
More trials; quantify error; compare control strategies; optimize competing metrics; proportional/PID-style concepts; competition extension; harder CV environmental variation.

## Absence recovery
Recover concept/evidence rather than copy notes: inspect/explain current subsystem, reproduce a key test, annotate code, complete individual explanation, or contribute to next iteration.

## Hardware sharing
Rotate physical robot testing, code/pseudocode review, notebook/data analysis and OpenCV/image work. Every student still receives meaningful physical robot experience.
