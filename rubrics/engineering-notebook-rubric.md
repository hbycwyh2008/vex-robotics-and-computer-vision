# Engineering Notebook & Portfolio Rubric

Use a 4-level scale for each dimension.

| Dimension | 4 — Exceeds | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaching | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Documentation frequency | Consistent dated evidence across sessions | Most sessions documented | Gaps between major sessions | Sparse or reconstructed at end |
| Technical detail | Setup, ports, code/mechanism details are reproducible | Enough detail to understand work | Important details missing | Vague descriptions |
| Testing evidence | Repeated trials, measurements, controlled comparisons | Appropriate tests with usable evidence | Limited or inconsistent testing | Claims mostly unsupported |
| Debugging process | Clear symptom→hypothesis→test→evidence→decision cycles | At least one well-documented debugging cycle | Fixes described but reasoning incomplete | Little debugging evidence |
| Iteration | Multiple evidence-based revisions with keep/revert decisions | At least one meaningful evidence-based revision | Changes made with limited justification | Changes are random/unexplained |
| Reflection | Specific insight about limits, trade-offs and next steps | Clear reflection on learning and next step | General reflection | Minimal or missing reflection |
| AI disclosure | Meaningful AI use is fully disclosed and verified | AI use disclosed and explainable | Disclosure incomplete | Undisclosed/unexplained AI-generated work |

## Scoring
- 25–28: Exceeds Expectations
- 19–24: Meets Expectations
- 13–18: Approaching Expectations
- 7–12: Beginning

Teachers may convert this rubric to the school's grading scale.
