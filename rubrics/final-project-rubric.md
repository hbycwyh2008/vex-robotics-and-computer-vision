# Final Integrated Robotics + AI Vision Project Rubric

| Dimension | 4 — Exceeds | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaching | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanical system | Reliable, maintainable and well matched to task | Functional and appropriate | Works inconsistently | Major mechanical issues |
| Programming/control | Modular, robust control with clear states/feedback | Required behaviors work and are explainable | Partial/inconsistent control | Major control gaps |
| Vision/perception | Stable perception with measured performance and clear limits | Required perception pipeline works | Detection unreliable or weakly tested | Vision largely nonfunctional |
| Integration | Perception, decision and action form a coherent tested system | Major subsystems integrate successfully | Integration only partially works | Subsystems remain disconnected |
| Testing & validation | Requirements traced to repeated tests including stress conditions | Repeated validation supports main claims | Limited validation | Mostly demo-based claims |
| Engineering iteration | Multiple evidence-based revisions with documented trade-offs | Meaningful iteration shown | Few revisions / weak evidence | Minimal iteration |
| Documentation | Architecture, code, data, build and limitations are reproducible | Required evidence is complete | Important evidence missing | Documentation inadequate |
| Technical defense | Team answers questions with evidence and understands system limits | Team explains design and results competently | Explanation has major gaps | Team cannot defend submitted work |
| Responsible AI use | AI assistance fully disclosed, verified and critically evaluated | AI use disclosed and understood | Disclosure/verification incomplete | Unexplained or misleading AI-generated work |

## Required final evidence
- requirements and architecture;
- robot/mechanism documentation;
- code/repository;
- perception pipeline;
- decision/state logic;
- repeated validation data;
- stress/failure testing;
- iteration/debugging log;
- limitation statement;
- final demo;
- AI-use disclosure.
