# Robot Build / Mechanism Rubric

| Dimension | 4 — Exceeds | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaching | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Structural quality | Rigid, aligned, clean, maintainable | Functional and generally reliable | Some looseness/misalignment affects performance | Major assembly problems |
| Mechanical function | Mechanism performs task repeatedly and smoothly | Mechanism completes required task | Works inconsistently | Rarely/never completes task |
| Parts/tool use | Correct components/tools used independently | Mostly correct use with minor support | Repeated support needed | Unsafe/incorrect use persists |
| Engineering reasoning | Design choices tied to constraints, force/speed/geometry trade-offs | Major choices explained | Some explanation but weak evidence | Choices unexplained |
| Testing & iteration | Multiple measured revisions improve performance | At least one tested revision | Limited testing or revision | Little/no iteration |
| Maintenance & safety | Wiring, fasteners and moving parts managed safely and neatly | Meets safety expectations | Occasional issues corrected after prompting | Safety/maintenance concerns remain |

## Evidence expected
- build photos/diagram;
- explanation of key mechanism;
- repeated performance tests;
- at least one iteration entry;
- final limitation statement.
